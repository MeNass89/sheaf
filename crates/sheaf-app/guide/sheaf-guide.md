# Sheaf Guide

Sheaf reads, marks up, edits, converts and signs PDFs on your computer. Nothing you open leaves it.
This guide walks through what you will use most. Help ▸ Keyboard Shortcuts lists every key.

## 1. Home and the Tools page

- **Open a PDF**: File ▸ Open (Ctrl+O, ⌘O on a Mac), drag a file onto the window, or pick one from
  **Recent files** on Home. Pin the ones you come back to; the search box filters the list.
- **Tools**: every tool is on the Tools page, by group: Organize, Optimize, Convert to PDF,
  Convert from PDF, Edit, Security. Click a tool, add its files, choose the options, then press the
  button at the bottom. The result opens in a tab, or in its folder for files Sheaf does not read.
- **Favourites**: the star on a tool keeps it at the top. Open Tool in the menu bar reaches every
  tool from anywhere, and Preferences ▸ Keyboard shortcuts can give each one its own keys.
- **History**: Home ▸ History lists what each tool made, with its files, so you can find a result
  again or run the same settings once more.

## 2. Reading

- **Move around**: scroll, Page Down and Page Up, or Space. Home and End go to the first and last
  page. Type a page number in the page box to jump there.
- **Zoom**: Ctrl+plus and Ctrl+minus, Ctrl+wheel, or a pinch. The zoom menu fits the page or its
  width, or shows it at actual size.
- **Layouts**: View ▸ Page Layout shows single pages, two pages side by side, a continuous column
  or pages across. **Split view** shows two places of one document, or two documents, side by side;
  their scrolling and zoom can follow each other.
- **Panels**: the sidebar holds the page thumbnails, the outline (bookmarks), comments, attachments
  and layers. Drag thumbnails to reorder pages.
- **Search**: Ctrl+F finds words in the document. Search catalog… searches several documents or a
  folder at once, with whole words, match case, AND / OR / NOT and words near each other.
- **Read aloud and night reading**: More ▸ Read aloud speaks from the page shown; Preferences ▸ Appearance
  darkens the pages or shows them in sepia.

## 3. Marking up

Each reader tool has a letter; press it while the pages have focus.

| Tool | Key | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| Select Text | V | Select and copy text |
| Highlight, Underline, Strikethrough | H, U, K | Mark the words you drag over |
| Note | N | A sticky note where you click |
| Pen | P | Draw freehand |
| Shapes | R | Rectangles, ovals, lines, arrows, clouds; measure distances and areas |
| Stamp | M | Approved, Draft and your own stamps |
| Eraser | X | Remove the marks you drag over |

Comments carry your name (Preferences ▸ Identity). The Comments panel lists them, sorts them by
author or type, and lets you reply to them and set their status. Summarize Comments prints them beside their pages.

## 4. Editing

- **Edit Text and Objects (E)**: click a line of text to change it in place, in its own font when
  the font allows it. Drag pictures and drawings to move them, use the handles to resize, and bring
  them to the front or send them back.
- **Add Image (I)** and **Link (L)** place a picture or a link where you drag.
- **Pages**: in the thumbnails, rotate, delete, insert, extract or replace pages. The Organize tool
  works on several files at once.
- **Undo**: Ctrl+Z undoes any change until you close the document. Nothing is written to the
  file until you save (Ctrl+S); Save As keeps the original as it was.

## 5. Forms and signing

- **Fill a form**: click a field and type. Tab moves to the next field. Auto-Complete offers what
  you typed before.
- **Prepare Form (F)** finds the fields of a flat form, or lets you add text fields, check boxes,
  lists and signature fields yourself.
- **Sign (S)**: draw, type or pick an image of your signature and initials once; Sheaf keeps
  them, encrypted, for the next time. Click where the signature goes.
- **Digital signatures**: with a digital ID (a .p12 or .pfx file, Preferences ▸ Signatures) you can
  certify or sign a document so any change after signing shows. The Signatures panel checks the
  signatures a document already holds.

## 6. Converting and optimizing

- **To PDF**: images, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, web pages, Markdown and e-mails. Word, PowerPoint
  and Excel files use LibreOffice when it is installed; Sheaf says so when it is missing.
- **From PDF**: images, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, web pages and text, and PDF/A for archives.
- **Compress PDF** makes a file smaller at the level you choose, or down to a size you give.
  Advanced settings choose image resolution and quality and remove what does not show.
- **OCR PDF** recognises the text of scanned pages so you can search, select and copy it. Review
  recognised text shows the words it is least sure of, one by one.

## 7. Security

- **Protect PDF** sets a password to open the file, and permissions for printing, copying and
  editing. **Unlock PDF** removes a password you know.
- **Redact PDF** removes text and areas for good: what is under a redaction is deleted from the
  file, not covered.
- **Security policies** save the protection you use often, to apply it in one step.

## 8. Doing the same thing again

- **Actions** chain several tools (for example compress, number the pages, then recognise text)
  and run them on many files at once.
- **Watched folder**: every PDF dropped in a folder you choose goes through an action by itself.
- **Library** keeps the documents you work with in one place and searches their text.

## 9. Preferences

Edit ▸ Preferences (Ctrl+comma) holds the appearance and language direction, the zoom and layout new
documents open with, rendering, your name, signatures and digital IDs, and keyboard shortcuts.
Every key in the menus can be changed there.

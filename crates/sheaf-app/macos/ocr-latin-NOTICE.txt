OCR model: latin-recognition.onnx and latin-dict.txt

PP-OCRv5 Latin text recognition model (latin_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec) and its character dictionary,
from PaddleOCR by the PaddlePaddle Authors. Reads 32 languages written in Latin script, accents included.
Source: https://github.com/PaddlePaddle/PaddleOCR
ONNX conversion: https://huggingface.co/monkt/paddleocr-onnx (languages/latin/rec.onnx, dict.txt),
revision 7b02d0a30a07ba2b92ad1ff5a8941ae2c633de65.

# Sheaf — brief

**The product.** A free, open-source PDF toolbox that runs entirely on your computer: every tool people pay
Acrobat, Smallpdf or iLovePDF for, with no account, no upload, no paywall.

**For whom.** Anyone who handles PDFs — students, freelancers, clinicians, small offices — and refuses to
upload contracts, IDs or medical files to a web service; plus AI agents, through the CLI and MCP server.

**Platforms.** macOS (Apple Silicon first), Windows, Linux. Rust core and CLI, Slint UI.

**Today (v0.2.1).** 28 tools, page viewer, CLI with `--json`, MCP server; tests green on three OSes.

**Done for the parity release (v1.0).** Every component of the board at 9/10 or better against its main
inspiration on function, aesthetics and performance; a viewer people would use as their daily PDF reader.

**North stars.** Function · aesthetics · performance, equal weight. Native, light, fast; measured.
